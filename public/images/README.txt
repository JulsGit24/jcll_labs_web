# JCLL Photography Images

Place your photography portfolio images in this folder.
Supported formats: .jpg, .png, .webp

## Naming Convention
To make them appear automatically on the Work page (based on the current default configuration), verify the `src/pages/Work.jsx` file matches your filenames, or rename your images to:

- photo1.jpg
- photo2.jpg
- photo3.jpg
...etc.

## Recommendation
For best performance, ensure images are optimized (compressed) and not larger than 2000px wide.
